DraftXI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026 · Operator: DJS Consult · Contact: support@draftxi.in

DraftXI is a free fantasy cricket game for friend groups. It uses points only. There is no real money, no purchases, no coins, no prizes, no advertising and no gambling. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos (other than an avatar you choose), health data, payment details or device advertising identifiers.

We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs or tracking technologies. If the app crashes, a crash report (device model, operating system and app version, and the technical error) is sent to Sentry so we can fix it; it does not include your name, email, chats or league data. We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking.

Cricket statistics come from a third-party cricket data provider. That provider does not receive your personal information.

2. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To run your leagues: drafts, rosters, lineups, waivers, matchups, standings and playoffs.
  • To show league chat and your notification feed.
  • To send push notifications about your leagues, if you allow them. You can turn them off in your device settings.
  • To keep the service working, fix bugs and prevent abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for marketing, or any automated decision that has legal effects on you.

When you report a chat message we store the report (reason, optional note, a copy of the message text, and who reported and who posted it) so we can review it, and we keep it as long as needed to enforce our terms. Your list of blocked members is stored with your account, is visible only to you, and is removed when you delete your account.

Other members of your leagues can see your display name, avatar, team name, and the chat messages and draft picks you make in those leagues.

We may disclose data if the law requires it. We will not otherwise share it with third parties.

4. Your choices and account deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile, then Delete account.

When you delete your account:

  1. Your sign-in account is removed from our authentication system. You can no longer sign in.
  2. Your profile is anonymised. Your display name is replaced with "Former manager", and your email address, avatar and push token are cleared.
  3. If you were a league manager, management passes to another member of that league. If no other member remains, the league is deleted.
  4. Your team stays in each league. It is no longer controlled by you. The app manages it with automatic substitutions, so other members' leagues, standings and results keep working.
  5. Chat messages and draft or lineup history you created remain in your leagues so that league history is not broken. They are attributed to "Former manager", and they are no longer linked to you.

If you cannot use the app, or want your data removed without deleting your account, email support@draftxi.in from the address on your account.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, we remove the sign-in record and clear the personal fields in section 4 straight away.
  • Anonymised league history (chat messages, picks, scores) is kept for as long as the league exists, so other members' leagues stay intact.
  • Database backups may keep deleted data for a limited period set by our hosting provider, after which the backups are overwritten.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and TLS. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security rules, so members can only see data they need for their leagues. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International users

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where UK or EU data protection law applies, you have the rights listed in section 8, and we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers, such as the standard contractual clauses used by our providers.

  • Contract: to provide the game and your leagues.
  • Legitimate interests: to secure and improve the service.
  • Consent: for push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data (see section 4).
  • Restrict or object to processing in some cases.
  • Port your data to another service.
  • Withdraw consent for notifications.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing of personal information (CCPA). We do not sell or share personal information.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

To exercise a right, email support@draftxi.in. We will respond within the time required by law, usually one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (for example the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the California Attorney General for CCPA matters).

9. Children

DraftXI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email support@draftxi.in and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date at the top and, for material changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
