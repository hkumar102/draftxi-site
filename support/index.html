Support

Questions, bugs or feedback: email ccm.hemantsharma@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile, then Delete account. Your profile is anonymised and your sign-in removed straight away. If you can't open the app, email ccm.hemantsharma@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Report abuse

Long-press a chat message to report it or block its author. We review reports within 24 hours.
